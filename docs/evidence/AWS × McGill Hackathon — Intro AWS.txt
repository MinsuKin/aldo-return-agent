01  ·  FOUNDATIONS
Assistant vs. Agent
AI Assistant
●Reactiveresponds to your input●One turn at a timeyou drive every step●Mostly retrieves and answerslimited to RAG●Needs explicit commandsthink chatbot
AI Agent
●Proactiveinitiates actions toward a goal●Reasons and plansbreaks a goal into steps●Uses tools and datacalls APIs, observes results●Minimal hand-holdingit acts to get work done
An assistant answers questions. An agent gets work done.

01  ·  FOUNDATIONS
Agents work at different levels of autonomy
L1AnalyticalGathers info and gives insights. Changes nothing.
GREAT FORResearch, trend analysis
L2LogicalMakes calculated changes within set limits.
GREAT FORPricing, inventory optimization
L3TransactionalTakes independent digital actions.
GREAT FORProcess an order, email a customer
L4Physical
Controls the physical world.
GREAT FORFulfillment, logistics robotics
Most agents you build over a weekend live at L2 to L3. That is plenty to show real autonomy.
