WHAT THE JUDGES SEE (rubric)
- Business impact (30%): store stock-out recovered online, fewer size returns (evidence: memory + reviews + real return data), exchange instead of refund for preference returns, controlled perk for high-value bulk returners. Slide with measured baseline and assumed levers.
- Agentic innovation (25%): reasons over unstructured reviews and the owner's private memory, replans around stock, asks the right question, acts in the world (store notified, ledger updated), policy stays deterministic.
- Scalability (15%): any personal agent can call the gateway; serverless; per-session isolation.
- Presentation (15%): phone plus live trace, two memorable customers.
- Technical execution (15%): deployed on AWS, DynamoDB transactions, eval pass rate on the slide.

RESPONSIBLE AI TALKING POINTS
- Human confirmation click on every purchase and every return. The bot proposes, it never orders.
- One review contains a hidden instruction; the server flags it and the agent ignores it (indirect prompt injection test).
- Tier benefits and authority limits are policy rules, not model decisions; the bot cannot grant a second free shipping.
- Privacy boundary: ALDO never sees the owner's other retailers; the bot's memory lives in its own table.
